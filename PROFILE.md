## ❌ Avoid these keyword traps

- Don't start with "student" or "AI engineer" — you'll get filtered out for senior embedded roles.
- Don't use "hobby" / "passion" / "passionate developer" — reads as a beginner.
- Don't lead with AI. Your real moat is **industrial CAN/MISRA firmware**; leading with AI undersells you and misdirects recruiters.
